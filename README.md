# Optical Persistence

After the 1.0 infusion update, an Optical Attraction with Hard-Light Prison's pattern can achieve permanent uptime as its cooldown scales below its duration. 

This mod gives a permanent buff that applies half of Optical Attraction's damage reduction to you while an Optical Attraction that meets the above conditions is equipped. 

Unlike other general damage reduction effects, this does not reduce the amount of element applied to the player to not nerf self-application builds. The effect of Optical Persistence is overridden while Optical Attraction is active so they do not stack.
